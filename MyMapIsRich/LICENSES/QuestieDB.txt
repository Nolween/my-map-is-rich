The quest data (Plus/Data/Quests.lua) is derived from the Forever database of QuestieDB
(https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), made by Logonz, Muehe, TheCrux (BreakBB), Drejjmit, Dyaxler,
Cheeq, TechnoHunter, Yttrium and everyone who sends data with QuestieTrace. The Questie team allows
copying it under the GPLv3 (the LICENSE file next to the toc); its licensing notice is below, as it is.
The list of quests never offered on Forever (removed, duplicated, holiday and war effort quests) is
read from Questie's own blacklist (Database/Corrections/QuestieQuestBlacklist.lua and Holidays/,
https://github.com/Questie/Questie), under the same licensing.
My Map Is Rich takes ids, levels, names, spawn positions and quest relations from it; it is not
Questie, and does not use its name or logo. Drop chances are not taken (QuestieDB has them from
Wowhead). The game content (names, titles) belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.

Because QuestieDB and the Questie content it was built from historically never had a proper license the situation is complicated and it is unlikely we will be able to gather consent from all the historic contributors for a specific license, so when in doubt consider QuestieDB content not covered above as "all rights reserved".

That being said, the Questie team considers historic contributions made in the spirit of open source development, although of course we cannot make that statement on behalf of all individual former contributors.

Hence the Questie team is alright with others forking the repository or copying code from it under the GPLv3 terms, but we can not promise all contributors are.

This does NOT extend to the Questie name and logo! Please change them when you maintain a fork.
